Trading

    
Minting and trading are separate. Trading on PancakeSwap opens only after the team adds liquidity. No date has been promised. Until then your CATAGENT stays in your wallet. To see it, add the contract address above as a custom token in your wallet.

  


  

    
Read this before you mint

    
    
      
  • The team holds 20% of supply from day one. This is public in the contract.
    • 
      
  • Your USDT is paid to the team wallet. The contract does not create a trading pool. The team adds liquidity manually later, and nothing in the contract forces it to.
    • 
      
  • There is no promise of a price, a listing date or any profit. You can lose everything you pay.
    • 
      
  • Only send USDT through the two calls above. Never transfer USDT directly to the contract.
    • 
      
  • Check the legality of crypto in your own country before you mint.
